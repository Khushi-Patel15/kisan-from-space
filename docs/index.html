      Read the maps with care

      • A prediction, not a survey. On test fields the AI names the right crop roughly half the time; it is best at wheat, maize, sugarcane and fallow.
      • Late-season images. The training photos were taken 20 March to 29 May 2022, when many crops were already harvested. Mustard and other early crops are easy to miss.
      • Regional bias. Each state was photographed on a different date and grows different crops, so the model partly learns the region's usual crops.
      • 10 m pixels, tiny fields. Half the surveyed fields are smaller than 0.25 ha (25 pixels), so field edges blur together.
